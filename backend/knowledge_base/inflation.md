# Inflation

## What inflation does
Inflation is the general rise in prices over time. It means a rupee buys less next year than it does today. The Reserve Bank of India targets consumer price inflation of 4%, within a band of 2% to 6%.

## Why it matters for savings
Money kept in cash loses purchasing power when inflation is higher than the interest it earns. A goal that costs ₹10 lakh today will cost more in five years, so long-term goal amounts should allow for rising prices.

## The rule of 72
Dividing 72 by an annual rate gives the approximate number of years for something to double. At 6% inflation, prices roughly double in 12 years. At a 12% return, money roughly doubles in 6 years.

## Real returns
The real return is the return after inflation. A fixed deposit earning 7% when inflation is 5% gives a real return of about 2%. Comparing options on real returns gives a truer picture of growth.

## In simulations
FinTwin simulations apply no inflation by default, so results are in today's prices. A scenario can include an inflation rate to see its effect on spending over time.
