# Credit scores

## What a credit score is
A credit score summarises how reliably you have repaid credit. In India, credit bureaus such as CIBIL produce scores from 300 to 900. Scores above roughly 750 are generally considered good and can lead to easier approvals and better interest rates.

## What affects it
- Repayment history: paying EMIs and credit-card bills on time matters most.
- Credit utilisation: using a large share of your credit-card limit lowers the score; keeping usage below about 30% of the limit helps.
- Credit mix and age: a long history of well-managed credit helps.
- Many recent applications: applying for several loans or cards in a short time can lower the score.

## Improving it
Pay every bill on time, ideally with automatic payments. Pay credit cards in full rather than the minimum. Avoid closing your oldest card unnecessarily, and check your credit report once a year for errors.

## Why it matters for planning
A better score can reduce the interest rate on a future home or car loan, which lowers the EMI and the total interest paid over the loan.
