# Insurance basics

## Insurance protects the plan
Insurance protects your savings and goals from events that would otherwise wipe them out. It is usually set up before investing for long-term goals.

## Health insurance
A medical emergency is one of the most common reasons families in India fall into debt. A health insurance policy, often a family floater, covers hospitalisation costs. Employer cover is useful but usually ends when you change or lose your job, so many people keep a personal policy as well.

## Term life insurance
If others depend on your income, term life insurance pays a sum to your family if you die during the policy term. It is the simplest and cheapest form of life cover. A commonly cited rule of thumb is cover of about 10 to 15 times annual income, adjusted for loans and future goals.

## Mixing insurance and investment
Products that combine insurance and investment often provide less cover and lower returns than buying the two separately. Understanding the cover, premium and lock-in before buying helps avoid surprises.

## Premiums as a planned cost
Annual premiums create a predictable spike in spending. Setting aside a monthly amount for them keeps cash flow smooth. This is general education, not advice on any specific policy.
