# FinTwin — AI-Powered Financial Health Digital Twin

> Analyze. Predict. Simulate. Decide Better.

FinTwin builds a derived "digital twin" of a user's finances from their transactions, profile, investments, loans and goals. On top of the twin it adds a transparent Financial Health Score, ML forecasting and anomaly detection, a deterministic What-If simulator, and an AI Financial Coach whose answers are grounded in the user's own data.
